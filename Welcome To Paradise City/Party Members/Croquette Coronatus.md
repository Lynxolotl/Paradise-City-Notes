# Backstory

## The War
Croquette would be drafted to serve in the oncoming war from Paradise City where he would be enlisted as a field medic. During his time in the military, he would become good friends with 3[^1] members of his squadron. One would go on to receive debilitating injuries later into the war and is still hospitalized presently. Another would go MIA and presumed dead, though Croquette and several of his squad mates hold out hope that he's still alive. The third is still enlisted in the military, now a captain of his own squadron continuing to fight on.
## After The Incident
Croquette would stay back to heal the other members of his squad, yet despite doing nothing wrong he blames himself for the state his friend is in. This incident would lead Croquette to make the rash decision of leaving the military to take more direct measures to end this war. He would cut communications with everyone he knew, even his dearest Orchis would be unaware of what he was about to do. With all ties to his home severed to ensure that if he fails, he won't be traced back to his home, he would travel to Paradise City with a vow to stop this cycle of violence at it's core.
